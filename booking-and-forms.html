Feedback Option

Written feedback is offered at the regular session rate of $100/hour ($90/hour if you've purchased a 4-pack). In the case of 4-packs, you may designate any portion of these hours for feedback.
‍
Meeting for a session is not required in order to receive feedback—you may simply opt to have your document reviewed/edited. If we've met for a session, the feedback option offers guidance on revisions made since that point.
